What this service involves

Prototype capabilities

Sensor selection, microcontroller evaluation, communications options, data logging, device-to-dashboard integration and proof-of-concept testing.

A staged development path

Define the use case and operating environment, research suitable components, build a limited bench prototype, test measurable requirements and plan the next engineering iteration.

Engineering considerations

Battery life, connectivity, enclosure design, electrical safety, privacy, reliability and any applicable Australian compliance requirements are evaluated for each project.
