What this service involves

What a feasibility review covers

Problem definition, comparable products and technologies, high-level solution options, assumptions, component availability, cost drivers and key technical risks.

Typical deliverables

A concise findings report, recommended proof-of-concept scope, open questions and a staged development plan appropriate to the available information.

Scope boundaries

Technical landscape research is not a legal patentability opinion, freedom-to-operate clearance or guarantee that an idea is novel or commercially viable.

Frequently asked questions

Is this a patent search?

We can review publicly available technical information, but formal patent searches and legal advice should come from a qualified patent professional.

Can you research an idea before a prototype exists?

Yes. Early feasibility work is intended to help determine whether prototyping is justified.
