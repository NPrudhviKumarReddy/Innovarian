What this service involves

Typical maintenance tasks

Updating text and images, checking important links and forms, reviewing mobile layouts, monitoring obvious errors and making agreed content changes.

How support is organised

After a redesign, when business details change, if enquiries stop arriving or when an existing site becomes difficult to update.
